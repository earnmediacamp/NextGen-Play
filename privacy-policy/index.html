NextGen Play Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

Summary

NextGen Play is a video player for files on your phone and links you type in yourself. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your video list, watch history, favourites, added subtitles and settings are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded. The app asks for permission to read videos on your device so it can list and play them, and for notifications so background play can show its controls. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything.

Features that use the internet

Network links: when you enter a link, the app connects straight to that server. That server can see your IP address and what you request. We do not run those servers.

Subtitle translation (optional): if you allow it, the text of subtitle lines is sent to Google's translation service so it can be translated. Video and audio are never sent. Google's privacy policy applies to that text. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy. Hinglish conversion happens on your phone.

Update check: the app contacts GitHub, where releases are hosted, about once a day to see if a new version exists and to download it when you choose. GitHub can see your IP address.

Ads and tracking

There are none. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first and you will be asked before anything is turned on.

Children

NextGen Play is not directed at children under 13. We collect no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can turn online translation off in Settings > Privacy, skip update checks by not using the updater, and delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
