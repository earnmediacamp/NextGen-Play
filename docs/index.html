What it does

Gestures

Swipe to seek, volume and brightness. Press and hold for 2x and slide to change the speed. Pinch to zoom and pan.

Subtitles

SRT, VTT and animated ASS/SSA. Delay, style, fonts and auto sync. Several speakers never overlap.

Hinglish translation

Translate subtitles into Hinglish, Hindi you can read in English letters. It asks your permission first.

Decoders

HW, HW+ and SW modes with automatic fallback, so more files play smoothly.

Audio

Equalizer, volume boost, audio delay and a remembered audio language.

Playback

Background play, picture in picture, resume, sleep timer and A-B repeat.

Library

Folders, search, favourites and a Continue Watching row.

Private

No ads, no accounts, no analytics. Your files stay on your phone.

How to install

  1. Tap Download above and wait for the APK to finish.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect asks to scan the app, choose to scan it or install anyway. It asks because the app is not on the Play Store.
  4. Open NextGen Play and allow access to your videos.

After this, the app tells you when a new version is out and updates itself from inside the app. You do not need to come back here.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is free software under the GPL-3.0 licence, and the source code is public.

Does it have ads or track me?

No. There are no ads, accounts or analytics. The only things that go online are the update check, links you enter yourself, and subtitle text if you choose to translate.

Why is it not on the Play Store?

It is shared directly from this page. That is why Android shows the install prompts above.

Does it come with videos?

No. NextGen Play does not provide, host or link to any video. It plays files on your phone and links you enter yourself.
